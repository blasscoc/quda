64^4 FP4 instrumented smoke test
================================
One leapfrog step, four fermion solves, two iterations per solve, tile 65536.
Diagnostics are enabled by default; --no-diagnostics disables timing wrappers
and the additional residual applications. Fixed solver iteration budgets remain
unchanged. Every iteration prints a live solver_iteration event.

Synchronized host wall times include GPU completion. Inclusive stage times
contain nested calls: do NOT add them together. Exclusive times subtract timed
children. First-use/JIT overhead is included in the first recorded calls.

Each iteration records total algorithm seconds, operator seconds, separately
measured residual-diagnostic seconds, recursive and recomputed relative residual,
alpha, beta, pAp and denominator validity. Recomputed residual uses the quantized
operator and quantized subtraction, not an FP64 reference. It costs one extra
operator application per iteration, excluded from iteration_seconds but included
in solve/trajectory stage times. No residual-based early stopping is introduced.

Convergence: all four solves ended with recursive residual 0.103847 and
recomputed residual 0.266503. Neither reached 1e-1, 1e-2 or 1e-3. The discrepancy
shows the quantized recurrence residual is an unreliable convergence indicator.
Proposal rejected, delta H +180288; zero denominator breakdowns. This is an
execution/diagnostic test, not validated sampling.
