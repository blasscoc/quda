CG and SGD crossed the requested stopping target with two-component FP4.
One-component FP4 did not cross it. Adam and tile-Muon also missed the target
within 24 updates in the two-component run. Their availability is not a claim
of convergence superiority. CG retained the lowest solve time to this target.

Adam sensitivity: an earlier epsilon=1e-8 smoke gave residuals ~822 (one
component) and ~241 (two) at 8 updates. Packed variance underflow with a tiny
epsilon can amplify updates. epsilon=0.01 reduced this problem but did not
reach the requested target. No learning-rate/epsilon sweep was performed.

Memory: state-MiB is exact persistent solver storage, excluding RHS and operator.
Torch peak includes model, RHS, outputs, native workspace and tile temporaries.
Prior backend/module cycles are garbage-collected between component settings.
Torch reserved memory is logged separately; whole-machine RAM is not sampled.
